## Review limits

All five selected images were viewed after generation or correction, and each PNG was decoded successfully. Their generated dimensions are 1672 × 941 pixels, except edited BOARD_14 at 1671 × 941. Image integrity and illustrated shot coverage do not certify exact likeness, anatomy, measurements, utensil hygiene, doneness or production acceptance.

The drawings capture one representative instant from each shot, including moments before a transfer completes; the screenplay's opening/end states still govern motion staging. Review cropped furniture positions, pan-handle direction, small butter quantities, hand anatomy and every raw/cooked transition against the continuity ledger before approving individual production first frames. The first pancake's two halves must continue to count as one through the later twelve-pancake serving sequence.
