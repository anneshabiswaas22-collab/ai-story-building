## Review limits

All five selected PNGs decode successfully at approximately 1672 × 941 pixels; an edited sheet can differ by one pixel. Source labels, character subsets and major cooking/serving states were inspected. The joined halves of the first pancake count as one throughout; wide compositions still need close per-frame review of fine cut seams, partially occluded food, hands, watch side and exact visual likeness. These studies establish illustrated coverage, not creator acceptance or proof of performed cooking/food safety. Use the screenplay, recipe and continuity ledger when staging individual production first frames.
