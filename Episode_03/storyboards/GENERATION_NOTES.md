## Inspection and production limits

Each selected sheet was visually inspected for four distinct labeled story moments, original cast/subsets, wardrobe, major bowl/vase states and kitchen geography. Known major errors were corrected through image generation. Cooking/serving panels must follow the [recipe and batch ledger](../../CONTINUITY.md): one batter batch, six cooking pairs, twelve small pancakes, two per family plate, adult-controlled heat and an explicit stove-off state.

Illustrated coverage does not certify exact likeness, anatomy, partially occluded ingredient/pancake counts, doneness or camera blocking. The original home-table preview includes decorative flowers; the Episode 03 meal compositions keep the food/table center clear rather than introducing flowers into the recipe. Review all details against the screenplay and exact editable overlays before accepting individual production first frames. A four-panel sheet is not a single motion input.

The original full-resolution identity/home PNG masters still belong to the owner's archive. New generated view PNGs are drafts rather than recovered originals. Voice, motion, animatic, final movie and creator approval remain separate production work; no automated Task 5 video or voice call was made for this package.
