# Maple House English — original 10-episode story ideas

**Each episode gets a 20-minute story first.** The creator may attach separate, custom learning/practice segments later, making the uploaded runtime 20, 25, 30+ minutes. These are *ideas*; only Episode 01 currently has a complete expanded screenplay. Episode 02–10 production folders remain intentionally empty.

Learning through natural dialogue and character decisions is part of the story. No forced audience pronunciation repetition, vocabulary documentary, quiz, timed learner pause or recap is planned in the first 20 minutes.

## Episode 01 — Dad Mixed Up BOTH Shopping Lists! | Vegetable Market English

**Story:** Maya plans salad and Nina plans soup. Dan combines their color-coded shopping notes, misreads quantities and assigns the right vegetables to the wrong dishes. The children help him ask, check and sort. Everyone cooks together.

**Comedy:** Four versus forty carrots; silent bell pepper; corn with ears; a salad suspiciously resembling soup.

**English naturally used:** carrot, tomato, cucumber, zucchini, potato, onion, broccoli, bell pepper, corn, lettuce, cabbage, spinach, basket, list, bag, receipt, change

**Story arc:** Confidence -> confusion -> admission -> teamwork -> shared meal.

**Cameos:** None; establish all six permanent characters.

**Production:** See Episode_01/ for revised story-only 20-minute screenplay.

## Episode 02 — Dad Bought the Sourest Fruit! | Fruit Shop English

**Story:** Dan wants perfect fruit for a family picnic. Max thinks sour means spoiled, and Dan chooses fruit by size alone. A tasting comparison and a budget challenge teach the family to describe what they want.

**Comedy:** A lemon is mistaken for a tiny yellow orange; the biggest fruit will not fit in the picnic basket.

**English naturally used:** apple, banana, orange, lemon, lime, grape, strawberry, watermelon, peach, pear, ripe, sour, sweet, peel, slice, pound, bunch

**Story arc:** Size contest -> sour surprise -> compare carefully -> fruit salad.

**Cameos:** None.

**Production:** Idea only; empty production folder reserved.

## Episode 03 — Flowers in the Pancakes?! | Kitchen English | Family Comedy

**Story:** Dan hears flour as flower while helping make breakfast. Maya catches the misunderstanding before anything is added. Nina turns the near-mistake into a kitchen scavenger hunt; the children measure correctly.

**Comedy:** An empty vase waits beside a mixing bowl; Dad asks whether pancakes need a bouquet.

**English naturally used:** flour, flower, bowl, whisk, spoon, spatula, measuring cup, batter, pan, stove, plate, egg, milk, butter, pour, stir, flip

**Story arc:** Misheard word -> check ingredients -> coordinated cooking -> imperfect but tasty breakfast.

**Cameos:** None; adults handle the hot stove.

**Production:** Idea only; empty production folder reserved.

## Episode 04 — Dad Tried On the Wrong Shoes! | Shopping Mall English

**Story:** The family shops for school shoes. Dan confuses his shoe box with Max's, then follows a map upside down. Maya's sister Rose appears briefly to help locate the fitting area, not to solve the whole problem.

**Comedy:** A giant adult foot next to a tiny shoe; Dad calls the escalator a moving staircase and keeps missing the sign.

**English naturally used:** mall, entrance, elevator, escalator, store, size, pair, fitting room, shelf, cashier, receipt, refund, tight, loose, comfortable

**Story arc:** Wrong box -> wrong direction -> ask clearly -> find the right fit.

**Cameos:** Aunt Rose, Maya's sister: 60-90 seconds.

**Production:** Idea only; empty production folder reserved.

## Episode 05 — The Picnic Is on the WRONG Bench! | Park English | Family Adventure

**Story:** Two similar picnic spots create a harmless mix-up. Dan follows a drawing without checking its orientation. The children use landmarks to find Maya and Nina while keeping together.

**Comedy:** Every bench looks like the bench in Dad's drawing; Max tries to ask a statue for directions.

**English naturally used:** park, garden, bench, fountain, path, pond, tree, flowerbed, gate, playground, blanket, picnic basket, shade, left, right, straight

**Story arc:** Wrong landmark -> careful observation -> clear directions -> picnic reunion.

**Cameos:** No new relative.

**Production:** Idea only; empty production folder reserved.

## Episode 06 — Dad Said HOT… But Meant WARM! | Restaurant English | Funny Family Dinner

**Story:** At a neighborhood restaurant Dan uses hot ambiguously. The server clarifies temperature versus spicy flavor. The family practices ordering, changing an order politely and asking for the check.

**Comedy:** Dan rehearses a grand speech, then only needs: Could I have some water, please?

**English naturally used:** menu, server, appetizer, main course, dessert, fork, knife, spoon, napkin, glass, mild, spicy, warm, order, check, takeout box

**Story arc:** Ambiguous order -> clarification -> enjoyable meal -> polite checkout.

**Cameos:** No new relative; server remains a minor supporting role.

**Production:** Idea only; empty production folder reserved.

## Episode 07 — Right Bus, Wrong Stop! | Travel English | Slow Family Comedy

**Story:** The family plans a library trip. Dan reads the departure time correctly but waits at the opposite stop. A quick call with Nina's father Ed helps them describe the direction, then they check the destination sign themselves.

**Comedy:** Dad proudly arrives early at the wrong side of the street; Max wonders whether a bus stop can move.

**English naturally used:** bus stop, route, ticket, fare, driver, seat, timetable, destination, arrival, departure, library, block, intersection, umbrella

**Story arc:** Good preparation -> wrong assumption -> verify destination -> reach the library.

**Cameos:** Grandpa Ed, Nina's father: 60-second phone cameo.

**Production:** Idea only; empty production folder reserved.

## Episode 08 — Grandma's Suitcase Has Everyone's Socks! | Home English | Family Story

**Story:** Maya's mother Ruth visits. Identical packing cubes mix up the family's laundry. Each person describes ownership and location; Grandma shares a gentle story about Dan's earlier packing mistake.

**Comedy:** Dad claims a tiny sock is a new travel-size fashion; Grandma produces a perfectly labeled checklist.

**English naturally used:** suitcase, zipper, drawer, closet, hanger, towel, pillow, blanket, socks, pajamas, laundry, pocket, label, charger, unpack

**Story arc:** Warm welcome -> mixed belongings -> ownership game -> family memory.

**Cameos:** Grandma Ruth, Maya's mother: 90-120 seconds, then off-screen family visit.

**Production:** Idea only; empty production folder reserved.

## Episode 09 — Can Dad Grow Money? | Garden English | Funny Learning Story

**Story:** The family plants mint, basil and vegetables. Max hears mint and imagines coins; Dan misjudges how much water a pot needs. They read labels, measure water and make a growth journal.

**Comedy:** A pretend paper coin is used as a plant label, not planted; Dad gives a tiny seed a very large name.

**English naturally used:** seed, soil, pot, root, stem, leaf, watering can, shovel, sunlight, shade, basil, mint, grow, dry, damp, label

**Story arc:** Wordplay -> overconfident gardening -> measured care -> first shared observation.

**Cameos:** Uncle Ben, Dan's brother: a 60-second delivery of empty pots.

**Production:** Idea only; empty production folder reserved.

## Episode 10 — The Party Cake Went to the Wrong House! | Everyday English | Family Finale

**Story:** A smudged house number sends a cake delivery to the neighbor. The family retraces events using descriptions, directions and polite questions. The neighbor returns the cake, and the family celebrates their teamwork.

**Comedy:** Dan has perfect party decorations but no cake; Max volunteers a very small strawberry as the whole dessert.

**English naturally used:** invitation, guest, neighbor, address, doorbell, balloons, ribbon, cake, candles, plate, decorations, delivery, mistake, explain, celebrate

**Story arc:** Party preparation -> missing cake -> reconstruct and ask -> warm payoff with callbacks.

**Cameos:** Brief returns of Rose and Ed; no large new cast.

**Production:** Idea only; empty production folder reserved.
