# Episode 01 — printed shot-label audit

This audit reads the visible badges from the actual PNG pixels with local Tesseract OCR and Pillow analysis crops. It checks panel order (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right), not just filenames or screenplay links. Each SHA-256 binds the result to the selected image. This does not certify likeness, story action or creator approval.

Refresh after changing a board: `python Episode_01/audit_labels.py`. The local tool requires Pillow and Tesseract; CI validates the saved labels and hashes with the standard-library episode checker.
