# Maple House English — four-slot Episode 1 storyboard production standard

**The original repository is the style authority.** Use [BOARD_01.png](../assets/storyboards/BOARD_01.png) and [BOARD_02.png](../assets/storyboards/BOARD_02.png) to inspect the intended picture treatment, not previously rejected 8-panel sheets.

**Sheet contract:** 1672 × 941 px, 16:9 landscape; **exactly four** cinematic 16:9 illustrated slots as **2 columns × 2 rows**, flush inside one sheet, fine white separators. Each slot fills its quadrant. In the **lower-left corner of each slot**, place a small translucent charcoal label with the **exact** `SHOT_###` ID in white. No wide white caption bar, frame title, poster headline, comic speech bubble, teaching recap or text inside the scene. Editable grocery-list/receipt lettering is supplied later as a separate overlay.

**Visual standard:** The established polished, friendly, naturalistic 3D animation stills in [existing board 01](../assets/storyboards/BOARD_01.png)—soft realistic light, credible lens composition, meaningful facial acting, natural anatomy and scale. Do not reinterpret skin color, face, haircut, uniform or ages. Each panel should be its own film still with precisely the actors and props the script specifies. No extra doubles or made-up actors.

**Locked actor portraits:** [DAN](../assets/characters/DAN_portrait.jpg) · [MAYA](../assets/characters/MAYA_portrait.jpg) · [NINA](../assets/characters/NINA_portrait.jpg) · [LEO](../assets/characters/LEO_portrait.jpg) · [ELLA](../assets/characters/ELLA_portrait.jpg) · [MAX](../assets/characters/MAX_portrait.jpg). For original full-quality PNG masters refer to the project ZIP; the JPEGs are inspection copies. [Continuity bible](../CONTINUITY.md) governs world and timing.

- Dan: **blue polo**, navy trousers, dark moustache, watch on anatomical left wrist.
- Maya: **plum shirt**, cream trousers, dark ponytail; Nina: **teal shirt**, beige trousers, wavy auburn bob.
- Leo: **orange shirt**, navy shorts; Ella: **coral shirt and denim overalls**, two braids; Max: **green shirt**, tan shorts.
- **Never add off-screen characters** to make a panel look fuller. Follow every panel's `Visible cast` exactly.
- Keep background locations faithful to the original master camera geography. Items cannot teleport or duplicate.
- Shopping notes: BLUE = Maya/salad; YELLOW = Nina/soup. No shopping items before they're bought, two totes remain empty until sorting.

**Production gate:** This is a script-aligned **image-generation brief**, NOT a completed illustrated storyboard. Only move a sheet into `Episode_01/assets/storyboards/BOARD_XX.png` after visually checking four correctly rendered slots, exact IDs, actors, geography, hands, lists, and prop inventory. Don't replace BOARD_01–12 and don't treat the brief as an image. Stop if reference identities drift.

**Work in batches of only one or two sheets** and seek creator review before expanding to further sheets, rather than generating eight sheets in one request.
