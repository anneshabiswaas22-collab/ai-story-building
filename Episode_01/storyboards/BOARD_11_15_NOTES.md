## Review limits

All five selected PNGs were visually inspected for the four correct current shot labels, recognizable original cast, major camera/action continuity, off-screen phone rules, tote transfers and visible change count, then decoded successfully. Exact small/occluded food quantities, hands, likeness, prop lettering and individual production-frame suitability still require creator review. Existing editorial SVGs govern readable list/receipt amounts; generated lettering is not the authority. These are illustrated composition drafts pending approval, not motion clips or accepted individual first frames.
