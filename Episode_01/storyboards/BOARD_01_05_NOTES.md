## Inspection and corrections

All five selected sheets were visually inspected for the four consecutive badges, distinct source moments, named cast, original wardrobe, two separate blue/yellow notes, empty tote state and selected-produce transitions.

- Board 01 preserves the blue salad note, yellow soup note, both adult partners and the three children's arrival.
- Board 02 preserves the four travelers only; Maya and Nina do not appear in the market. The basket is empty and the two totes are folded/empty.
- Board 03 keeps the basket empty through SHOT_012 and uses the correct basket handoff, bag hook, handle recognition and carrot-number clarification.
- Board 04 shows four carrots, then three protected tomatoes. Selected food is distinct from display stock; no cucumber is bought yet.
- Board 05 distinguishes the unpurchased zucchini from the cucumber. A focused correction removes a duplicate cucumber from the SHOT_019 basket while the single cucumber is still held for transfer; SHOT_020 shows that same cucumber inside the basket.
- Basket bodies were unified to natural tan wicker with a blue handle, preserving the two separately colored cloth totes. No fruit-shop cooler or pancake props were introduced.

The selected images decode successfully as PNGs at approximately 1672 × 941 pixels; localized edits may differ by one pixel in width. These checks establish illustrated draft coverage, not creator approval or exact production-frame certification. Partially occluded food counts, hand anatomy, likeness and exact prop lettering still require close review against [continuity](../../CONTINUITY.md) and the original SVG overlays before motion production. The blue/yellow note headings in studies do not replace the authoritative editorial list artwork.

Each panel is a representative frozen story moment, not an entire fifteen-second sequence or an approved motion first frame. The original generated attempts and selected sources remain preserved in the generated-images directory; old selected storyboard files remain recoverable from Git history and the root's pre-rebuild archive.
