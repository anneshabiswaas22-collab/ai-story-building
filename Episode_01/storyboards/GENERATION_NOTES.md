# Episode 01 — regenerated sequential storyboard provenance

The active package contains **20 newly generated four-panel PNG sheets**, with **80 consecutive shot illustrations**. BOARD_01 covers SHOT_001–SHOT_004, BOARD_13 covers SHOT_049–SHOT_052, and BOARD_20 covers SHOT_077–SHOT_080. Every intervening sheet follows the same four-shot rule and reading order: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right.

All twenty sheets were generated from the rebuilt screenplay using the actual original cast and environment previews as visual references. The earlier partial boards were not used as image-generation inputs, renamed to fill slots or appended to a new sequence. Their historical versions remain recoverable from Git history before the rebuild; the current folder contains only the selected twenty-sheet sequence.

Browse the [complete gallery](../../STORYBOARD_GALLERY.md), [HTML gallery](index.html) and [shot/panel index](../../STORYBOARD_INDEX.md). The [printed-label audit](SHOT_LABEL_AUDIT.md) reads the badges from the selected image pixels and binds the observed numbers to each PNG's SHA-256. The episode checker also verifies those saved labels, hashes, image uniqueness and source links.

## What was reviewed

Each sheet was visually inspected for four distinct current shot labels and matching story moments, original cast/subsets, major prop/inventory state and geography. Market frames show Dan and his three children; Maya and Nina are heard only as off-screen phone voices and do not appear in call insets. The vendor stays off-screen/hands-only. There is one woven basket and two tote bags, with their transfers governed by [CONTINUITY.md](../../CONTINUITY.md).

The printed-label audit verifies visible numbering, not story semantics, likeness, hand anatomy, exact occluded food counts, cooking doneness or creator approval. These illustrations are composition drafts. Review the screenplay and exact editable overlays before staging and approving individual motion first frames. A four-panel sheet is not a single-shot motion input.

No animation, performed voice, lip-sync, animatic or final film is claimed. Original full-resolution identity/environment PNG masters still belong to the owner's archive. Task 5 motion/audio work and creator approval remain separate from this regenerated written/still-image handoff.
