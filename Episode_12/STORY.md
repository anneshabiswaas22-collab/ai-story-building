# Episode 12 — Dad’s Shoe Is Going to School!

A Maple House English family comedy in 80 consecutive 15-second shots. Story runtime is 20:00.

Leo wants two pictures for a page he’s taking to school. The first is easy. At four, he photographed the first bow he ever tied. Dad had to stand very still inside the shoe. For the second, Leo wants all six Brookses in the frame. A yellow cushion gets into the family portrait before he does.

## Cast and setting

Dan, Maya, Nina, Leo (12), Ella (9) and Max (7) wear their usual W01 clothes. At the dining table, face the rear window. Dan sits near-left, Leo at the left end, Ella far-left, Maya far-right, Nina at the right end and Max near-right. Leo’s little album has two clear pockets and fits in his schoolbag beside his chair.

In the backyard the house door is rear-left and the shade tree rear-right. The family’s compact camera stands on a tripod on the open lawn facing the tree. From behind its lens, Leo’s place is front-left, Max’s front-center and Ella’s front-right. Dan, Maya and Nina form the back row from left to right. Leo has a clear walk around the tripod on the camera’s left. Close shots can pick out two people from the group.

The four new pictures are digital. The compact stays attached to the tripod from shot 015 through shot 070. A small printer in the kitchen makes the one new paper print. The old brown shoe in the album and the green sneaker on Max’s right foot have different-colored laces.
