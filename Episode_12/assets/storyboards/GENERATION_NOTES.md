# Episode 12 storyboard sources

The four-panel sheets illustrate shots from [Dad’s Shoe Is Going to School!](https://github.com/arunabanarjee32-bit/ai-story-building/blob/9ce74370da8e0799baf4528f7e7d5f872f62b46e/Episode_12/STORY.md). The [gallery](../../STORYBOARD_INDEX.md) links each panel to its timed screenplay shot.

## Image direction

Each PNG was generated for its four consecutive shots using the original adult and child lineups. The direction specified a warm, stylized 3D image, four equal landscape panels in reading order, the named actors and their W01 wardrobe. Each panel brief included the screenplay’s camera direction, action and continuity. The pictures are composition drafts for creator review.

The additional references for each sheet are listed below. Reference boards supplied a recurring prop or supporting character design. The screenplays establish each panel’s location, cast and prop state.

## Sheet 08 generation direction

Leo’s walk, the late pose and the saved elbow use the same camera and yellow cushion as sheet 07.

<details>
<summary>Image prompt for shots 029–032</summary>

> Use case: illustration-story. Continue the existing Episode 12 board, using reference 1 as the base sheet: replace its four panels with the NEXT four shots (029–032) to make BOARD_08. Reference 1 establishes the animation, compact camera on black tripod, cushion, house and garden. References 2 (adult lineup: Dan/Maya/Nina) and 3 (child lineup: Leo/Ella/Max) control facial likeness, age, proportions and clothes. Deliver just the finished wide 16:9 four-panel contact sheet: exactly two columns and two rows, four equal landscape frames, very thin warm-white cross gutter, no heading, speech, numbers, watermark or labels. Warm, polished stylized 3D film stills, with the soft, late-afternoon sun, bright grass and mottled shade of the reference. Preserve W01 outfits and faces: Dan sky-blue polo and mustache, Maya plum tee dark ponytail, Nina teal blouse auburn bob, Leo 12 orange tee and navy shorts, Ella 9 coral tee and braided hair with denim overalls, Max 7 green tee with tan shorts.
>
> TOP LEFT (029): a wide side view shows two seconds were too short. Near-left a compact on tripod facing the family; Leo walks around its left side, orange right sleeve and elbow just entering the camera's line, still well short of the spot. In the garden Dan, Maya, Nina are standing in that order, Max and Ella in front with a plain square yellow cushion lying flat in the empty front-left spot. Stage Leo's motion clearly, quiet action.
>
> TOP RIGHT (030): from just above the lens, full family portrait after the shutter. Exactly six, three grown-ups back (Dan blue, Maya plum, Nina teal); three children in front from left Leo orange kneeling on the flat yellow cushion, Max green, Ella in overalls. Leo now looks straight ahead with a deliberately perfect smile; Ella suppresses laughter. This one is a real scene, full-bleed; do not include the camera in this frame.
>
> BOTTOM LEFT (031): close lateral detail: black tripod-mounted compact on playback and a child's fingertip (Ella) pointing to the VERY LEFT EDGE of its LCD. Emphasize the visual joke. In the SCREEN image five tiny recognizable faces ONLY (back blue Dan, plum Maya, teal Nina; front Max green and Ella in overalls). AT EXTREME LEFT of the saved photo the only trace of Leo is a clipped sliver of orange sleeve and bare right elbow. The live Leo standing beside the camera holds up his own bent right elbow to match, so its shape rhymes with the screen. Keep the LCD readable and large.
>
> BOTTOM RIGHT (032): intimate eye-level 3-shot at tripod: Dan bends toward the boy's elbow, fist held as pretend microphone, then Leo lowers his arm and meets Nina's patient eye, quietly disappointed that his face missed the picture. Dan's teasing softens. Keep the same sunny garden, let Leo's expression carry the ending. No literal microphone. Four different editorial compositions, natural hands.

</details>
