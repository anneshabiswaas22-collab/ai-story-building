## Inspection scope and remaining work

Known sheet-specific issues are logged in SOURCE_RECORD.md; a visual draft is not silently treated as accepted continuity. Generated panels can compress camera blocking, omit small props or drift across fixed restaurant seats. Exact faces, fingers, jewelry, LEFT-watch/RIGHT-pocket sides, six glass counts, menus after collection, particular food portions and cash denominations require corrected individual frames and creator review. A correct PNG checksum does not certify these visually.

The screenplay and continuity ledger govern six choices, no food before service, one ticket, gradual eating, one saved portion into one box and exact payment arithmetic.
