# Episode 06 — Dad Said HOT… But Meant WARM! | Restaurant English | Funny Family Dinner

> **Format:** 80 continuous story shots × 15 seconds = **20:00 story runtime**
>
> **Status:** written and illustrated preproduction draft. Creator approval, individual motion first frames, performed voices and video remain pending.
>
> **Story-only:** natural General American family comedy. Optional creator-authored practice belongs after the story; no audience drills, quizzes, narrator recap or timed repetition.

After their park outing, the Brooks family has a relaxed restaurant dinner. Dan arrives with a grand speech and an imprecise request: hot soup. A server asks whether he means temperature or spice. The family clarifies six individual choices before anything is cooked, enjoys the meal, saves one remaining portion and pays a carefully checked bill. The humor comes from a speech that is never needed and a small question that is.

## Story arc

- **00:00–02:00:** plan dinner and an all-in $80 limit; Dan rehearses too much.
- **02:00–04:00:** arrive together, keep six fixed places, ask simply for water.
- **04:00–09:00:** choose six mains; distinguish warm temperature from spicy flavor before submitting one ticket.
- **09:00–12:00:** give cooking real time, receive the correct dishes and check comfortable temperatures.
- **12:00–16:00:** enjoy dinner, pack only Max's existing remainder, ask for the check.
- **16:00–20:00:** verify $63.80, pay $80, receive $16.20, leave $10 tip, retain $6.20 and depart together.

## Performance and continuity rules

Exactly six main courses are ordered once; no appetizer or dessert is purchased. Food subtotal $58.00; posted fictional tax 10% = $5.80; meal check $63.80. Cash payment $80.00; returned change $16.20; voluntary tip $10.00; retained remainder $6.20; final cost $73.80. Six plain still waters are free; no chargeable drink or refill is added. The same SERVER remains an off-screen voice or hands/forearms-only helper; no new permanent character face is designed. Left aisle side near-to-far: Dan, Leo, Ella. Right window side near-to-far: Max, Nina, Maya. Nina packs only Max's existing leftover portion once into one box; Max's plate is empty afterward.

Use the unchanged W01 cast and original face/age/body references. Dan's watch is on anatomical LEFT wrist; phone/wallet/rehearsal note remain in his RIGHT pocket. Maya and Nina share decisions and authority. SERVER is explicitly off-screen/hands-only and never appears as a new face. Actual editable overlay text governs prices, not generated lettering.

Mild describes flavor, while warm/hot describe temperature; one does not prove the other. Adults check comfortable temperatures before children eat. There is no burning-mouth gag, forced spicy taste, mistaken cooked order, wasteful return or new allergy plot. Washing/cooking/serving/eating/travel need explicit story-time ellipses. No food duplicates or refills across cuts. See [CONTINUITY.md](CONTINUITY.md).
