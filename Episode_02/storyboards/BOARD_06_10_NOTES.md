# Episode 02 — illustrated boards 06–10 provenance and review

These five actual generated PNG sheets contain twenty illustrated composition studies for SHOT_021–SHOT_040. They are **visual drafts pending creator review**, not approved motion first frames, lossless original character masters, generated video or recorded voices.

Each sheet is 1672 × 941 pixels, arranged top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right, with white gutters and a SHOT badge. The images were created using the image-generation tool and copied unchanged from its saved outputs. Python was used only to inspect file dimensions, decode integrity and calculate checksums; no substitute illustrations or visual edits were drawn in Python.

## Limits before motion production

These panels illustrate the intended beat and composition, with some basket contents obscured by hands, rims or protective trays. A still cannot establish fruit weight, actual freshness or a perfectly conserved moving inventory. Read exact weights/counts from the screenplay/continuity ledger, then inspect every individual production first frame and take. The source JPEG references preserve the existing cast for these draft illustrations; the owner-controlled full-resolution PNG identity masters and creator likeness review remain prerequisites for production-grade approval.

No creator approval, per-shot motion first-frame acceptance, lip-sync review or paid video generation is claimed by this file.
