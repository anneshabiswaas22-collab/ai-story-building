## Review scope

Every selected sheet was visually inspected for its four labels, distinct story actions, recognizable named cast, wardrobe and major prop/state errors. Detected missing people, duplicated selected fruit, mismatched blanket color, explanatory inset diagrams and incorrect staging were corrected through image generation rather than replaced by written placeholders.

These checks establish actual illustrated coverage, not exact production-frame approval. Occluded fruit counts, grape-cut geometry, fine currency details, hand anatomy, likeness and shot-to-shot blocking still need close review against [CONTINUITY.md](../../CONTINUITY.md) when staging individual motion first frames. The screenplay and exact SVG overlays govern quantities and readable text. A four-panel sheet should not be uploaded as a single motion input.

Original full-resolution identity/kitchen PNG masters still belong to the owner's archive. New generated environment PNGs are new visual drafts, not recovered original masters. No voice, animation, render job or finished film is claimed. Creator review and the separate Task 5 authorization remain outstanding for production.
