## Review limits

The selected sheets were visually inspected for shot labels, named-cast counts, broad likeness/wardrobe, the single divided card, empty basket, supported unpurchased watermelon and sample-versus-purchase state. The missing-character and sample-rind defects above were corrected through the image-generation tool and the replacements were inspected.

These are representative story-action compositions. A panel is not automatically an exact opening frame for its shot, nor proof of perfect anatomy, immutable facial likeness, exact hidden prop counts or production-ready geometry. Camera crops differ between panels; final production still needs original full-resolution reference masters, shot-specific staging, creator review, exact editorial prop text and individual first-frame approval. No new character designs or actor/voice approvals are claimed.
