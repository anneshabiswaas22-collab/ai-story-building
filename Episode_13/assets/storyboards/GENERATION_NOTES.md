# Episode 13 storyboard sources

The four-panel sheets illustrate shots from [Do I Get My Duck Back?](https://github.com/arunabanarjee32-bit/ai-story-building/blob/9ce74370da8e0799baf4528f7e7d5f872f62b46e/Episode_13/STORY.md). The [gallery](../../STORYBOARD_INDEX.md) links each panel to its timed screenplay shot.

## Image direction

Each PNG was generated for its four consecutive shots using the original adult and child lineups. The direction specified a warm, stylized 3D image, four equal landscape panels in reading order, the named actors and their W01 wardrobe. Each panel brief included the screenplay’s camera direction, action and continuity. The pictures are composition drafts for creator review.

The additional references for each sheet are listed below. Reference boards supplied a recurring prop or supporting character design. The screenplays establish each panel’s location, cast and prop state.

## Sheet 07 generation direction

Ella keeps June’s magnifier as she walks to the bench. The striped top stays on the felt, and the duck and blue case stay in June’s tote.

<details>
<summary>Image prompt for shots 025–028</summary>

> Use case: illustration-story. Replace the four panels of the supplied Episode 13 sheet 06 with the next four screenplay moments (025–028). Reference 1 controls Mabel, June, her tote, the girls, light, the room and the green-handled magnifier. References 2 and 3 are the established Brooks adults and children in lineup order Dan/Maya/Nina and Leo/Ella/Max. A warm, lived-in neighborhood swap room, polished original 3D family animation, daylight, rounded expressive faces with the same skin tones and proportions. As in the prior sheet, a 16:9 wide contact sheet of exactly four equal 16:9 frames in a two by two grid, thin ivory dividers. No lettering, numbers, overlays or speech bubbles.
>
> TOP LEFT, 025. At Ella’s eye level beside the green-felt swap table. Ella (two braids, coral tee, full-length blue denim overalls) has stopped mid-sentence, holding one green-handled magnifying glass to her chest. Plum-shirted Maya with dark low ponytail and orange-shirted Leo listen without crowding her. Over Maya’s shoulder we can see the short unobstructed aisle to the bench by the blue door, where June in mustard sits small and out of focus. Stage Ella's moment of realization clearly.
>
> TOP RIGHT, 026. Medium tracking shot along the aisle; Ella walks toward the bench, carrying the green magnifier in one hand, the other held palm-back at waist height to say “I’ve got it.” At the table behind her, Dan (mustache, blue polo) gives a small two-finger salute; Nina (auburn bob, teal top) nudges the red-and-white striped wooden spinning top farther onto the green felt. Keep the toy at the table. Warm oak floor; daylight from high windows.
>
> BOTTOM LEFT, 027. At the end of the wooden bench, exactly two girls: Ella sits down beside June, with June in a mustard hoodie, dark tight shoulder-length curls and small freckles, dark pants, as in reference. June sets the off-white canvas tote on the FLOOR BETWEEN their shoes and opens it enough for just Mabel’s small yellow wooden head with its orange beak to peek above the rim. The blue hinged pencil case stays inside. Ella still holds the circular green magnifier. June is beaming at having made a friend.
>
> BOTTOM RIGHT, 028. Closer still two-shot in profile, both girls seated, facing each other; Ella balances the green magnifier across her own knees, explaining gently. June looks surprised and thoughtful, reaches toward her blue case inside the tote on the floor, duck still inside it. They are still sorting out a misunderstanding. Keep their hands and props apart. Make the emotional shift from enthusiasm to uncertainty unambiguous. Match June's design from reference. No extra children or duplicated objects.

</details>
