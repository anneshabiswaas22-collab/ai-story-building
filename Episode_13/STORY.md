# Episode 13 — Do I Get My Duck Back?

A Maple House English family comedy in 80 consecutive 15-second shots. Story runtime is 20:00.

Ella takes two toys to the Saturday swap. Anyone can trade for her striped top. Mabel, the wooden duck with a funny knock, is coming home. Then a girl with a magnifying glass asks, “Want to switch?” Ella is still looking for Mabel’s noisy wheel when the yellow beak disappears into the other girl’s bag.

## Cast and setting

The Brooks family wear their usual W01 clothes. Ella (9) painted Mabel years ago. Leo (12) and Max (7) know the duck by sight. By the end of the afternoon, Dan will know her by ear. Maya and Nina come along to the neighborhood swap. June, a girl Ella’s age with dark curls, a mustard hoodie and a canvas tote, has come to find a sound that will stump her dad. June’s parents stay outside the frame.

At home, keep the oak kitchen island, the right-hand entry bench and the blue front door. In the community room, the long table stands under a window, a few steps from a bench inside the entrance. Face the window from the aisle. Ella uses the left end and June the right. Green felt covers the window side of the tabletop. There is a bare wooden strip along the aisle.

Mabel is yellow with four red wheels and an old thumbprint on one cheek. A dab of dried yellow paint on the rolling edge of her own left rear wheel lifts her tail once per turn. The wooden top has red and white stripes. Nina’s plain silver tin has a lift-off lid. June’s smaller blue pencil case has a hinged lid and holds her green-handled magnifying glass at the start. The swap takes place before lunch. The cut in shot 071 carries the story into the afternoon.
