# Episode 15 storyboard sources

The four-panel sheets illustrate shots from [The Book With Two Beginnings!](https://github.com/arunabanarjee32-bit/ai-story-building/blob/9ce74370da8e0799baf4528f7e7d5f872f62b46e/Episode_15/STORY.md). The [gallery](../../STORYBOARD_INDEX.md) links each panel to its timed screenplay shot.

## Image direction

Each PNG was generated for its four consecutive shots using the original adult and child lineups. The direction specified a warm, stylized 3D image, four equal landscape panels in reading order, the named actors and their W01 wardrobe. Each panel brief included the screenplay’s camera direction, action and continuity. The pictures are composition drafts for creator review.

The additional references for each sheet are listed below. Reference boards supplied a recurring prop or supporting character design. The screenplays establish each panel’s location, cast and prop state.

## Sheet 07 generation direction

The picture-book close-up shows three blue crosses on the green patch. The final panel keeps the giant fingertip inside the watercolor illustration.

<details>
<summary>Image prompt for shots 025–028</summary>

> Use case: illustration-story. Replace the previous Episode 15 sheet's four frames with the next shots, 025–028, in the sunlit library. Reference 1 establishes family on a window bench plus the green picture book, its blue-black beetle, twig, crumb and watercolor style. References 2 and 3 lock the adult and child faces and wardrobe. Reference 4 is the library itself. Exactly four wide film stills on one 16:9 landscape sheet (2 columns by 2 rows), extremely thin ivory dividers, no titles, stickers, numbers, lettering or speech bubbles. Polished stylized 3D characters and room; the drawings on the actual open, cream-colored book pages are visibly flat ink and whimsical watercolor with subtle paper grain. Distinguish real fingers from drawings. Picture book continuity: a SINGLE inverted hollow soft yellow hat floating like a boat, underside of its crown open to the sky like a bowl, brim as rim. It has a little green cloth repair patch on its brim with precisely three clearly separated BLUE X-shaped cross-stitches. Not a second hat, not a conventional boat hull. Beetle is small blue-black and carries a golden oat crumb.
>
> TOP LEFT, 025: close across an actual open book in Maya's lap. Paint a twig on the bank meeting the near yellow brim. A small ink beetle holding his oat crumb steps from the twig ONTO the floating yellow hat's edge, a gray dry stone still ahead across the water. At the page edge, Leo in orange traces the boarding action with a hovering finger, little Max in green purses his lips in a quiet “toot,” Maya's plum sleeve holds the book. Warm bookish wonder.
>
> TOP RIGHT, 026: an insert of the printed yellow brim with its ONE green patch holding exactly three blue X marks in a row, big and countable. Ella's hand in the foreground holds the separate old cream bookmark beside the page; its upward-facing end bears a faint child's pencil picture of a yellow hat. Max's finger hovers to count the crosses. Maya steadies the green-side book. Keep the bookmark away from the stitches. No writing or invented glyphs.
>
> BOTTOM LEFT, 027: medium close of Maya (plum shirt, dark low ponytail, gold studs) on bench, intrigued; she traces the hat's hollow contour in midair, while Leo in orange considers the possibility and Nina in teal with a short auburn bob watches. Frame the loose small bookmark beside the large open illustrated spread as two separate scales of drawing. The SAME green-side spread remains open; do not flip the book or reveal the other cover.
>
> BOTTOM RIGHT, 028: over Maya's plum shoulder, next watercolor spread fills the panel. Low beetle’s-eye painted viewpoint from inside the floating yellow hat as it nears the dry gray stone. In the illustration, a gigantic softly washed, outlined fingertip descends from the sky toward the beetle guarding one oat crumb. Crucial: the fingertip exists ONLY as a painting in the book and is bound by its page. At the physical book's outer margin Max in green curls his empty fingers protectively; Ella peers with curious concern. No real hand descending into the page. A subtle page shadow and center gutter distinguish printed interior from the room.

</details>
