All four newly generated mall PNGs were visually inspected. Each is a coherent warm landscape illustration with no people, large readable walkways and the requested palette. Entrance/directory/elevator/escalator/store landmarks recur; fitting and checkout share oak shelves/sage furniture. Generated perspective decoration and small object placement are design drafts, not final architectural geometry. The measuring-device form and exact sightlines need creator art-direction review before production. Use the editable MALL_GEOGRAPHY.svg as the story's route authority; the family never rides either elevator or escalator.

The selected clean images contain no exact authoritative shop/receipt/map text. Exact lettering, prices and route diagram are separately editable SVG overlays. Stock shoes in the backgrounds are unpurchased display stock and do not add to the family's one-pair purchase ledger.
