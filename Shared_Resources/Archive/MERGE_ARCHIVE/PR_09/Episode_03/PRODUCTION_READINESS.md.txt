## Task 5 — remaining motion and audio sequence

1. Review the screenplay and recipe/batch ledger; resolve dialogue pacing and approve the story before treating it as a script lock. Subtitles currently estimate timing only.
2. Restore and verify the original six identity PNG masters, reference sheets and home-kitchen/table masters. Preserve likeness, proportions, clothing and geography; shared viewing JPGs are useful references but not lossless production masters.
3. Review/correct the three new clean kitchen-view designs and the populated panels against [SCENE_REFERENCE_PLAN.md](SCENE_REFERENCE_PLAN.md). Preserve the environment and storyboard source notes; do not overwrite existing original references.
4. Review all 80 illustrated concepts, resolve faces/hands/portion/stove/seating issues and stage or extract **80 accepted individual motion first frames** with exact starting states. A four-panel sheet is not a single motion input.
5. Obtain the creator's separate explicit Task 5 instruction before automated video/voice generation. Verify actual account/model, supported duration, reference/voice features, permissions and displayed cost; no credentials or account capability are invented here.
6. Run the proposed 90-second story pilot and action-specific measuring/flip/serving tests. Inspect first/middle/last frames, actor identities, empty vase, one blue batter bowl, raw/cooked tool separation, heat handling and natural dialogue. Reject identity or inventory drift instead of silently adopting it.
7. Produce the remaining shots in logged, reviewable batches only after pilot acceptance. Preserve six pairs/twelve cooked pancakes, the one-tablespoon pan-butter reserve, stove OFF and six two-pancake servings across edits.
8. Assemble exactly 20:00 of story, conform subtitles to accepted voices, mix, verify technical properties and watch end-to-end. Creator-designed practice may be appended independently afterward; movie release needs separate acceptance.
