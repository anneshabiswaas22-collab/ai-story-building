## 3. PREP_ISLAND — two bowls become one batch

- **Clean visual draft:** [PREP_ISLAND.png](assets/environments/PREP_ISLAND.png), the same oak island with the established kitchen behind it. Clean unpopulated reference geometry stays separate from the stage-specific ingredient and actor arrangements.
- **Representative composition:** Maya supervises children measuring level dry ingredients in the cool zone. Nina handles raw eggs separately; Dan checks the recipe and helps whisk/fold. Children remain away from raw-egg handling, heated butter and knives. All six wash/dry hands at SHOT_021 before preparation.
- **Exact props:** One **BLUE dry bowl**, one **YELLOW wet bowl**, clean measures and utensils. Two cups flour, two tablespoons sugar, one tablespoon baking powder and half a teaspoon salt enter blue. Two eggs, one-and-a-half cups milk and three tablespoons cooled melted butter enter yellow. The fourth tablespoon of butter is reserved for the pan.
- **State variants:** Empty bowls; complete dry mix; complete wet mix; yellow emptied into blue; one blue batter bowl with yellow empty aside; covered/resting batter. Show the explicit five-minute story-time rest without a viewer countdown. No raw tasting, duplicate wet bowl, new batter batch or flower appears.
- **Review targets:** One frozen moment per illustrated panel, not a diagram showing full raw ingredients and their duplicate fully prepared state at the same instant. Exact measurements and quantity labels come from the saved recipe SVG. Preserve the blue bowl as batter moves to the stove.

## 4. STOVE_STATION — adult-controlled heat and six pairs

- **Clean visual draft:** [STOVE_STATION.png](assets/environments/STOVE_STATION.png), the existing rear-right stove and adult workspace, with a safe inward pan handle. The background reference is unpopulated and does not prove a burner is hot in a story frame.
- **Representative composition:** Nina and Dan perform the cooking with a controlled spatula flip close to the pan. Maya may assist with the clean cooked tray/serving; children stay at the cool island or table, never reaching around a hot adult.
- **Exact cooking ledger:** Six batches of **two** small pancakes: cooked totals 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. One tablespoon reserved pan butter is used incrementally across those batches. Keep raw batter ladle/whisk apart from clean cooked-food utensils.
- **State variants:** Adult melting/cooling the three-tablespoon batter portion; later pan preparation; first crooked but properly cooked pair; later pairs; twelve pancakes complete; empty batter bowl/raw tools aside; burner explicitly OFF after the final pair. - **Review targets:** No child touching stove/knife/hot pan, no airborne pancake, no burnt discarded food, no thirteenth pancake or growing duplicate stack. Golden color is not the only doneness cue; the adults verify a cooked firm center. Do not take the hot skillet to the dining table.
