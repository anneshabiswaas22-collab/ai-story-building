# Episode 11 — The Mouse That Ate the Moon!

Max has written a shadow play. All his mouse needs to do is walk up to the moon and ask it to dinner. Then Ella says “a little closer,” Dan moves the puppet toward the lamp, and one enormous ear swallows Max’s dinner guest.

## Video handoff

1. Pick a sheet in the storyboard gallery. Each sheet covers one minute in four shots, read top left, top right, bottom left, bottom right.
2. Open the shot link beneath the image. The matching prompt gives the camera, visible and off-screen cast, start and end positions, action, exact lines and clip-local speaker timings. The end of shot 080 falls at 20:00.
3. Use the indicated panel to compose one clean 16:9 frame for the clip. Some sheets have taller panels. Keep the actor, puppet and shadow in frame when reframing. The creator reviews the individual frame before motion generation.
4. Let the performers finish the physical joke. In shot 023, hold long enough to notice both tips of the moon disappear. In 065, stay on Max while he waits for the answer. In 080, leave a quiet beat after “Company.” Subtitle times are provisional until the recorded performance is available.

Max is seven, and Pip has his ordinary speaking voice. Ella is nine and voices the moon. Dan supplies a squeak in rehearsal, then lets Max decide when the giant mouse returns. Maya spots the moment the laughter stops being fun for Max. Nina runs the light and listens from the audience. Leo makes the distance marks and discovers the knock on the frame.
