## Task 5 — prerequisites and sequence

1. Review the written story and exact fruit/money ledger. Resolve any dialogue pacing issues before treating it as a script lock; the current SRT is a planning estimate.
2. Restore and verify the original six PNG identity masters, reference sheets and home-kitchen master from the owner's archive. Preserve likeness, wardrobe and body ratios. Shared viewing JPEGs are useful for discussion but do not establish lossless production inputs.
3. Review/correct the **four generated clean location drafts** and populated storyboard compositions against [SCENE_REFERENCE_PLAN.md](SCENE_REFERENCE_PLAN.md) and the [environment provenance notes](assets/environments/GENERATION_NOTES.md). Obtain creator acceptance of the new designs without presenting them as restored original masters. Do not overwrite existing kitchen or character sources.
4. Review all **80 illustrated concepts across the 20 four-panel sheets**, resolve known likeness/hand/fruit/seating issues, and stage/approve 80 individual camera-specific motion first frames. Preserve the existing PNG sheets and their generation notes as draft provenance. A whole sheet is not a motion first-frame input, and the presence of an illustration does not establish its acceptance.
5. Obtain the creator's separate explicit Task 5 instruction before automated video/voice generation. Check current account model, duration, reference features, voice permissions and real cost; no signed-in account or credits have been tested here.
6. Generate/review the proposed 90-second kitchen pilot, then action-specific samples. Check first/middle/last frames, unique actors, watch/pocket sides, one card, price arithmetic, family roles and audible natural speech. Reject drift rather than silently redesigning a character.
7. Only after acceptance, produce remaining shots in logged batches, with the giant watermelon staying unpurchased and safe preparation/cooler states intact. Preserve rejected/accepted take provenance.
8. Assemble the story to 20:00, retime subtitles against real dialogue, mix and watch end-to-end, verify technical export properties, and obtain release approval. Creator-authored practice can be appended independently afterward.
