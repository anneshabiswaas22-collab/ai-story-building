# Maple House English — production bible v1

## Creative lock

Working series name: **Maple House English**. Episode 1: **Two Lists, One Dad: The Vegetable Market Mix-Up**. The original fictional Brooks family lives in Maple Falls. Dan is married to Maya and Nina; Maya is Leo and Ella's mother, Nina is Max's mother. Do not silently change those relationships into an ex-wife arrangement. Both wives are equal, warm partners. The comedy comes from wordplay, overconfidence, ambiguous language and repair. Each family member has agency and useful ideas. Avoid jealousy, humiliation and accents used as punchlines.

The six individual `*_ID_v01.png` files are the selected visual masters. They have been created for this project and are the v1 production choice, **not a claim of explicit user approval or of tested video consistency**. Never overwrite v1. A requested future redesign becomes v2 with an explicit migration list of affected shots. Keep v1 throughout Episode 1.

## Identity versus state

Identity is stable: face silhouette, skin, eyes, hairline, hairstyle, age, body proportions and signature details. Current state can change: pose, facial expression, hand props, location, wet/dry food, basket contents and emotional tone. Outfit W01 is fixed throughout Episode 1. There are no alternate outfits in this episode. Future outfits must be derived from the same master while preserving the face and body; changing clothes is not a new actor.

## Visual language

Original polished stylized 3D, rounded forms, expressive but moderately proportioned heads, matte fabric, soft bounced light, natural hair volume and readable silhouettes. No named-studio imitation. Adults remain adults; children retain their ages. Hands are simplified but anatomically plausible. No photographic skin, extreme anime proportions or unexplained material changes.

Frame: 16:9. Planned delivery: 1920×1080, 24 fps, exactly 28,800 frames / 1,200 seconds. Generated source resolution is recorded separately; scaling 720p sources to 1080p is not native 1080p detail. Confirm available model/source options in Flow next prompt. Lens language: approximately 35 mm for room/market masters and 50 mm for conversations; close food inserts around 70 mm equivalent. These are visual direction, not a claim the generator exposes physical lens controls. Keep camera still or use one slow movement. No whip pans or frequent cuts during beginner listening.

Palette: ink #16332C, cream #FFF8EA, sage #7D9B80, sky blue #65A8D7, plum #733F69, teal #177C79, orange #D97528, coral #E97360, leaf green #4C8A43. Exact text and layout colors are controlled in the SVG assets; generated materials may vary slightly under light.

## Props and food continuity

Maya's **BLUE** list and tote are for salad. Nina's **YELLOW** list and tote are for soup. Dan reverses their purposes in his memory; the actual printed headings never swap. He buys the correct items but needs help assigning them to dishes. The notes overlap until the reveal. Phone and wallet use his right trouser pocket; watch stays left. Use `learning/props` for exact list, receipt and price artwork; do not ask the image model to typeset them.

One roomy woven shopping basket holds selected produce. Max carries it only while empty; Dan carries it once filled. Ella carries the two folded empty totes. The baskets/totes should not multiply or change material. Potatoes go below protected tomatoes. Cabbage and zucchini are examined and returned to their crates; they are never bought. Green peppers stay at the stall. No raw potato eating, thrown food or damaged produce.

All count-critical close inserts use the exact inventory graphics, or a separately checked image; illustrative storyboard produce is not authoritative inventory.

Payment: fictional prices total $18.50. Dan pays one $20 bill and receives one $1 bill plus two quarters ($1.50). Use simplified clearly fictional play-money artwork and the readable price overlay. Keep money flat on the counter; do not animate detailed currency morphing. No real purchase occurs in this production session.

After return: hands and produce are washed; adults prepare food; an explicit time ellipsis allows cooking. Children remain away from blades and the hot stove. The final table contains prepared soup and salad, not whole raw shopping props. Two flat list cards may remain at the table edge as callbacks.
